6Key Findings
  • ◆ Views are heavily right-skewed — a small number of videos go viral while most stay modest.
  • ◆ Likes show the strongest correlation with views (r = 0.84) among all engagement metrics.
  • ◆ Category significantly affects views (ANOVA, p < 0.001) — Entertainment dominates trending videos.
  • ◆ Weekday vs. weekend upload timing shows a statistically significant difference in views.
  • ◆ The regression model explains 57.4% of the variance in views using only engagement metrics.
